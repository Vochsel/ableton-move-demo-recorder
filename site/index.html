Every module, on a Move.

Each demo shows the module's real Schwung screen, drawn by Schwung's own display code, while the module's own DSP plays the notes and knob moves you see. Two cuts of each: the screen up close, and a 3D Move.

Videos generated from Schwung's renderer and each module's released DSP. Modules that need samples, ROMs or a network stream play on stand-in instruments (OB-Xd + 8W8), noted in the player.
